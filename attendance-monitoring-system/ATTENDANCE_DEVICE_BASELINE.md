# Registration and attendance devices

Registering on a PC and verifying email or signing in on a phone does not bind an attendance device or earn a device-change penalty. Login history remains recorded. The first QR submission that passes the event, checkpoint, QR, GPS, geofence, eligibility and login-device checks establishes `attendance_device_hash` and its timestamp. A pending shared-device submission also establishes this baseline; it has passed location and QR checks but still needs an administrator decision. Failed and duplicate scans and manual entries do not establish or move it.

After a baseline exists, a different browser identifier can create one device incident and one penalty point. Repeated login/check-in requests do not repeatedly add points. Administrator-trusted or dismissed incidents stay resolved unless new risk evidence appears. Browser identifiers use signed cookies; they are not physical MAC addresses. Clearing cookies, private browsing, or switching browsers may require review.

Shared-device detection uses actual attendance ownership from active accounts, rather than every browser ever used to sign in. Retired accounts and login-only devices are excluded. A new account using another active attendee's browser gets an incident and attendance review without a first-registration penalty. Repeating that same submission/login does not later charge the deferred point. Genuine new device changes after the baseline retain the existing one-point policy.

Existing baselines are backfilled from each active account's earliest approved or pending-review QR attendance with GPS. The migration refunds only unreviewed, open automatic penalties created before that first attendance (or for accounts with no attendance). Each refund records the original incident and refunded amount in the audit log. Login-only incidents are dismissed; genuine shared-attendance cases remain open with zero first-registration points. Historical login and attendance records, pending attendance decisions, and incidents already reviewed by an administrator remain unchanged.

`tests/attendance-device-baseline.sql` uses rollback-only fixture accounts and the deployed service role. It covers PC signup and phone verification, invalid location, first valid scan, later changes, idempotent penalties, administrator trust, real versus login-only sharing, retired ownership, manual attendance and service-only permissions.
