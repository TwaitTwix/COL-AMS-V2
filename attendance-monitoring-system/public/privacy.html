Privacy notice & consent

Version 2026-11-17

Why your data is used

COL AMS uses your information for campus account and student ID verification, event eligibility, attendance recording and reports, device and geofence verification, security incident review, and administrator accountability. Data collected through this system is intended only for these attendance and security purposes, not advertising or unrelated tracking.

What is recorded

Your name, student/personal ID, email, institution, college, course, role, account approval and attendance records are stored. Authentication credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. The system records browser device identifiers, browser details, login times, IP addresses, device-change flags and associated review/penalty records. A browser identifier is not a hardware MAC address.

When you submit QR attendance, the browser requests your permission to capture your GPS latitude, longitude and accuracy. Successful submissions retain that location, device identifier, distance from the venue and timestamp. Rejected verifications retain the reason and attempt count. GPS is an estimate with reported accuracy; it is not a guaranteed exact location. This system does not continuously track your location or capture GPS merely to sign in.

Manual attendance records the student, event, time, staff member and reason. It does not claim that the student's GPS or device was verified. Camera access is used to decode QR codes in your browser; this attendance flow does not upload camera video or photographs.

Who can access it

Authorized administrators and COL officers can view attendance, login/security logs and account-specific activity for the purposes above. Only the main administrator can promote or demote administrators. Supabase and Vercel provide the database, authentication, storage and hosting infrastructure. Report downloads use temporary private links. The system logs administrator actions, including account activity views.

Your choices and rights

You may decline consent and sign out without using the attendance workspace. You may request access, correction, or clarification of your data, object to processing, ask about withdrawal of consent, and request deletion where applicable through your school's administration or designated privacy officer. Contact your school about an alternative attendance arrangement if you cannot use a device or do not wish to submit GPS. Withdrawal or deletion requests must be reviewed against any applicable school recordkeeping obligations.

Retention and privacy contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact leonardolllmacalinaoisap@gmail.com. The school's official retention period has not yet been supplied for this notice. Ask campus administration for the approved retention schedule. The application currently retains records until an authorized school process removes them; it does not yet automatically delete records on a schedule.

Consent

The unticked consent checkbox asks you to agree to the collection and use described here for campus attendance and account security. Your acceptance time and this notice version are recorded. Consent does not waive your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.
